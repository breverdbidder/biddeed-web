## A2. Daniil campaign analysis

**Offer:** Cashflow Developer Academy (CDA) by Daniil Kleyman / Rehab Valuator (True Vision Analytics, LLC). Dallas-software + Richmond-VA development operator.

**Headline:** Build Attractive, High Quality, Easy to Own Properties That Pay You For Life

**Price (launch-4 page)**
- Anchor $6,000
- Launch $3,700 or 2 × $1,950
- $700 annual renewal after 12 months, cancel anytime
- 30-day unconditional refund
- Partner/spouse seat free
- Bonuses stacked to “$18,900 value” (events $5k, 911 1:1 $1.2k, design guides $3k, partner seat $3.7k)

**Delivery:** 3–4 courses / 110+ lessons; resource library; bi-weekly calls with Daniil; deal-help forum; guest trainers; Richmond live events 2–3×/year; “Near Me” member directory.

**Founder claims:** $110M personally developed; about-page $60M+ owned + similar pre-development; group stats 200+ developers, 50+ markets, $200M+ projects developed.

**Funnel pattern:** VSL launch page, scarcity (first 100 spots, live counter), value stack, dual beginner/scaler FAQ, mid-ticket checkout. URL slug `launch-4` = recycled launch.

**Grade from analysis**
- Offer-market fit B+
- Copy B (identity strong, $50k/mo overreach)
- Proof C− (no student artifacts on LP)
- Price psychology B
- Page craft C (typos, “4 vs 3 courses,” broken spot counter)
- Risk to buyer medium
- Ethics yellow (real operator, guru packaging)

**Not a scam.** Working developer + software company since ~2011. YouTube ~68k. Course dumps exist at $39–$142 — paid value is live community.

## A4. Competitor proof strategies

Proof rank that development buyers actually use:
1. Artifact (named building, photo, unit mix)
2. Underwriting artifact (sources/uses, stack)
3. Student artifact (their site, their lender, their CO)
4. Peer authority (named developers)
5. Crowd (member count)
6. Origin story
7. Media/TV
8. Guarantee
9. Emotional identity
10. Academic credential

**Katie:** catalog of named buildings (Loom, Dechiro, Marco Polo, Piccolo, Grappolo, Carini, La Vita) + named student projects + public math ($175k lot + $795k build → $1.35M sale). Best *transfer* proof.

**Sean:** named operators with firms (Ridgeview, Oak Investment); $200M; cohort cap 50; 14-day refund. Best *status* proof. No student CO on LP.

**Pace:** industrial student theater (19-year-old six figures, $1M gross / $3M acquired in 6 months). Best *momentum* proof. Velocity deals, not buildings.

**CDA:** founder process on YouTube + bio dollars + 30-day guarantee. Worst on-page transfer proof in the direct set.

**Minimum CDA proof upgrade:** 10 member project cards (template delivered in chat).

## A5. Project-card template (summary)

Same skeleton every card: photo, product type, stage pill, name, one-line, specs, **one number only**, stack, status, attribution.

Stages: Under contract | Entitled | Under construction | Leasing/sold | Stabilized | Founder.

Banned numbers: projected IRR, $50k/mo, bonus-value math.

Mix: 2 founder + 2 contract + 2 permitted + 2 construction + 2 leased/sold. Six real cards beat ten fake ones.

Full spec lives conceptually in `/workspace/artifacts/cda-project-card-template.md` from this thread.
