Florida has 67 counties and no two clerks run sales exactly alike. Different platforms, different deposit rules, different payment deadlines, different docket habits. A county playbook is the local knowledge layer you add on top of the statewide process from the 101s. BidDeed's [county pages](/counties) carry the live data per county — upcoming sale counts and calendar links — and these playbooks carry the operating notes.

First, the platforms you will actually bid on:

- **RealForeclose.com** — the dominant platform for Florida foreclosure auctions; most county clerks run their judicial sales here.
- **RealTaxDeed.com / RealAuction** — the common platforms for tax deed and tax certificate sales in many counties.
- **County-run portals** — the largest counties run their own systems (Miami-Dade, below). Always confirm the platform on the clerk's site or the county page before registering to bid.

Registration, deposits and payment deadlines are per-platform and per-county. None of them are forgiving about missed deadlines — the standard penalty is a forfeited deposit and suspension from future sales.

## Miami-Dade

The state's biggest and most competitive market.

- **Tax deed sales** run on the county's own **RealTDM** platform (miamidade.realtdm.com), administered by the Miami-Dade Clerk of Courts — not on the usual third-party platforms. Review the Clerk's administrative orders and the Tax Deed Unit's published sale calendar before your first sale.
- **Foreclosure auctions** run through the Clerk's online foreclosure sale system (miami-dade.realforeclose.com).
- **Volume cuts both ways.** More lots than anywhere else in Florida, and more bidders on every one of them. Discipline on the ceiling matters more here, not less — the room is deep.
- **Diligence notes.** Condo-heavy inventory means HOA and condo-association foreclosures are common — re-read the senior-mortgage trap in Foreclosures 101 before touching them. Municipal and code liens in Miami-Dade municipalities are notorious; never assume the wipe rule covers them without checking.

## Suwannee

The other end of the spectrum: a small north-Florida county where the docket is thin and the competition thinner.

- **Tax deed sales** run online at **suwannee.realtaxdeed.com**, governed by Florida Statutes 197.122 and 197.502–603; the Clerk of the Circuit Court (Live Oak) acts as auctioneer and posts the sale schedule and notices.
- **Foreclosure auctions** run at **suwannee.realforeclose.com**.
- **Small-county rhythm.** Fewer sales per month, sometimes none. The edge here is coverage: being set up and ready when a good lot appears, because there may not be another for weeks. BidDeed alerts on the county do the watching for you.
- **Diligence notes.** Rural parcels: landlocked lots, mobile homes (check whether the home is real property or titled separately), well/septic, and thin comp sets. Max-bid math needs conservative exits — the buyer pool on resale is smaller.
