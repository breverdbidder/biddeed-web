BidDeed Academy teaches two things, in plain English:

1. **The auctions.** How Florida tax deed sales and foreclosure auctions actually work — the process, the timelines, the due diligence, and the traps that cost new bidders real money.
2. **The tool.** How to use BidDeed.AI — the auction calendar, the property pages, the SIGNAL$ Max Bid, and Deed, the AI agent — to find, price and win the right lots.

No prior auction experience assumed. Everything here is education, not advice — read the disclaimer at the bottom of every page.
