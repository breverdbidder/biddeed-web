BidDeed.AI is auction intelligence for Florida foreclosure and tax deed sales: it reads the county dockets, prices every lot, and tells you what to bid — before the sale, not after. This page is the product tour. (Everything here works on the free tier; deeper modules are marked as they come up.)

## Start at the calendar

The [auction radar](/radar) is the spine: every upcoming foreclosure and tax deed sale across Florida's 67 counties, in map, table, calendar and spreadsheet views. This is your event-time advantage — county sites each publish their own list in their own format; BidDeed normalizes all of them into one docket that stays current as sales are added, postponed and canceled.

Use the calendar to answer "what sells this week, in my counties?" — then switch views to answer "where are those properties, actually?"

## Filter to a shortlist

[Discover](/discover) searches the auction inventory by county, case, address or sale type. A workable weekly rhythm:

1. Pick one or two counties you understand — the [county pages](/counties) show coverage and upcoming sale counts per county.
2. Filter the upcoming sales to those counties.
3. Open every property in the list, briefly. Kill the obvious nos fast: wrong area, wrong property type, homestead premium, visible deal-breakers.
4. What survives is your shortlist — and the shortlist is where real diligence starts.

## Read a property page

Each property page is the lot's dossier: the sale details (date, county, case number, opening bid), the plaintiff and case posture, the parcel data, and BidDeed's analysis — including the SIGNAL$ verdict and max-bid intelligence. Free accounts see a max-bid **range** on previews; the exact SIGNAL$ Max Bid is an Investor feature.

Two readings to practice on every page:

- **What is being sold, precisely?** Legal description, parcel, property type, homestead status.
- **What is the exit worth?** Comps, condition, zoning (Pro accounts run full ZoneWise analysis — setbacks, height, land use, permitted uses — on every lot), minus repairs and holding costs.

## Ask Deed

Deed is BidDeed's AI agent — on the [homepage](/) and in the chat drawer — and it answers against the live county record rather than a training memory. Use it the way you would a sharp analyst sitting next to you: "What's selling in Brevard next Tuesday?", "Walk me through this case number," "What survives the sale on this HOA foreclosure?" It is the fastest way to turn a docket line into an understanding.

## Set alerts so the docket works for you

Sales move. Clerks cancel, postpone and reset constantly, and finding out at the podium is expensive. [Alerts](/alerts) tell you when a sale you watch changes — date moves, cancellations, status flips — so the calendar you priced is the calendar you bid.
