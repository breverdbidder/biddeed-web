The website

1

Step 1: The home page

First impressions: clean, readable, nothing broken.

Open biddeed.ai

You should see

  • A big headline about the best prices in US real estate being set at foreclosure and tax deed auctions.
  • A chat box that says Ask about any Florida auction…
  • Text you can read without zooming, and no sideways scrolling.
  • Tap the dark mode switch at the top. The page should turn dark and stay easy to read.

Deed, the AI assistant

4

Step 4: Ask Deed a question

The heart of the product: a chat that answers like Claude does.

Open biddeed.ai/chat

Try this: Type What survives a tax deed sale in Florida? and send it.

You should see

  • The heading What are we bidding on?
  • The answer writes itself out on screen, usually within 30 seconds.
  • Now clear the box and type just /. A menu of skills pops up, such as /liens and /comps.

Step 5: Get to the payment page

The path a real customer takes to buy.

Open biddeed.ai/buy-report

Try this: Pick any county, tap any auction, enter your email, then tap Get my SIGNAL$ Property Report — $25.

You should see

  • The county list loads with upcoming auctions and dates.
